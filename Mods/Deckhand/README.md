# Astrolabe Deckhand

Looks after the passive skills. Built on Astrolabe Loader. **F3** opens it (rebindable in the F1 Mods menu).

- **Ranching:** pets animals that want it; repairs enclosures below a maintenance percent you set, keeping a GP reserve; tells you (and Beacon) what is short when it can't. Pet all and Repair all buttons.
- **Grain mill:** pick its product (stops, switches and restarts it for you); keeps the mill stocked from the bank (per grain: on or off, and how many to leave in the bank); restarts the mill when it stopped because it ran out (a mill you stopped yourself stays stopped).
- **Farming notices to Beacon:** automation or auto-plant can't be paid for, harvest blocked by a full bank, compost ran out. Once every 10 minutes per notice at most.
- A log of everything it did this session. Settings per character in `mods\Deckhand\chores`.

Nothing runs during offline catch-up.
