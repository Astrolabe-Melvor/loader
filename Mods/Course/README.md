# Astrolabe Course

Do one action until a goal is reached, then the next. Built on Astrolabe Loader. **F6** opens it (rebindable in the F1 Mods menu).

## Tabs

- **Add a step:** every field narrows the next. Goals only include what fits the action (item goals only if it makes items, "materials run out" only if it uses them), the item list holds only that action's products and drops with your bank count, level goals list only the skills it trains, and amounts start from where you are now. A line under the form says what the step will do and warns about anything that would stop it.
- **Picking:** pick a skill and an action (searchable, with icons), or "Copy what I'm doing" to fill in the action your character is on now. "Fastest XP" trains the skill with the best action you can do, switching as you level. Goals: it stops by itself, I own N of an item, I get N more, a skill reaches a level, N minutes pass, done N times. "Sell from bank" sells an item down to an amount you keep (locked stacks are never sold).
- **Plan an item:** works back through the recipes to what must be gathered, picks the fastest action for each, and adds the steps. Steps that need a higher level get a "train the skill" step in front. Items no skill makes come from the shop when it sells them (a "Most GP: any skill until you have N GP" step, then a "Buy from shop" step that buys packs until the bank holds enough; item costs such as Arena Reward Tokens are planned too), otherwise from a monster that drops them. Currencies the queue can not earn, such as slayer coins, are flagged. Most GP steps can also run "until I have N GP". Rates come from the Astrolabe wiki's `plan.json`, downloaded once a day and kept for offline use.
- **Saved and shared:** keep queues under a name; copy a queue as a share code or add steps from a pasted code. The Astrolabe wiki planner's "Copy for Course" button makes these codes too, and in Astrolabe Tools "Send to Course" adds the steps straight to your queue (through `mods\Course\inbox`).
- **Weapon and stance (combat):** each combat step can also hold a different weapon and fight in a set stance. Every weapon deals the same damage type; what differs per monster is its defence bonus against each stance (Stab, Slash, Crush, Block, Accurate, Powerful, Long Range), and the weapon decides which stances you can use. The form shows the monster's defence per stance and the best weapon and stance you own for it. "Best I own against it" picks both when the step starts, scoring every usable weapon in the loadout's style with the game's own hit chance, max hit, protection and attack speed formulas (magic is not ranked: the spell decides the damage). The game saves what you wear back into a loadout when it swaps away, so the queue remembers the loadout's own weapon and puts it back when a later step uses "loadout's own" or the queue ends.
- **Toolbelt sets (skills):** every skill has its own toolbelt. In the Toolbelts tab, make named sets per skill (start empty or from what is on now), pick an item for each slot, and give consumables a refill amount. A step can put a set on when it starts. When a consumable with a refill amount runs out during the step, the queue takes more from the bank, or, if there is none, plans that many (crafting or gathering whatever they need), runs those steps first, then goes back to the step with its progress kept and puts the new stock on. Toolbelt items an action requires (a Filtered Vial for Warped Cacti) are put in from the bank automatically.
- **Combat gear:** each combat step can wear one of your combat loadouts (gear, food, prayers, spells), so Melee for one monster and Magic for the next. "Combat default" uses the loadout picked under Options; "whatever is on" leaves it alone. Skills keep their own toolbelts, so this only affects combat.
- **History:** what the queue did, with times, kept between sessions.
- **Options:** start over at the end; sound and taskbar flash when it finishes, skips a step or pauses; stop combat when the food runs out; pause when the bank is full; corner panel; swap combat loadouts per step; a default combat loadout.

**From the game:** every skill and combat page gets a **Queue** button in its top-right corner. It opens Add a step with that skill and the page's selected recipe (or what's running there) picked. It can be switched off under Options.

## The table

Drag rows to reorder, double-click (or Edit) to change a step. Tick the box on several rows to move, drag or remove them together; **Tick all** ticks every step. **Remove finished** clears done and skipped steps, and the option "Take steps off the list once they reach their goal" does it as you go (not while the queue loops). Item goals show the time left at the current pace. While the queue runs the tabs fold away; click one to bring it back.

**Agility:** each path of a course is its own action ("Jump 3: Advanced Path"), so a step runs the path you picked; "(best path)" lets Course pick the fastest one you can run. Agility steps can also run until the bank holds (or you gain) a number of Agility Marks.

**Idle action:** what to do while a step waits for materials, and after the queue ends.

The game runs one action at a time, and so does the queue: one step after another. The queue also runs while the game catches up on time away: steps that reach their goal while you are gone move on to the next, and History shows when each one happened. Alerts and sounds wait until you are back. "Slayer rules all the time" and the Thieving and Agility food switch only run while the game is open.
