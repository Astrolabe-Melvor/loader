# Astrolabe Logbook

What you gained this session and how fast. Built on Astrolabe Loader. **F7** opens it (rebindable in the F1 Mods menu).

## Tabs

- **This session:** XP gained per skill, XP per hour (last 10 minutes; hover for the whole-session rate), levels gained, time to the next level at the current pace, and actions or kills. Below that, every item gained or used with a per-hour rate, and each currency (GP, slayer coins) gained and spent.
- **Notable drops:** items you got for the first time this session, and rare ones: items that came in at most once per 40 actions of what you were doing, with the rate you actually got ("about 1 in 230 kills"). "Only rare drops" hides the rest.
- **Past sessions:** the last 60 sessions with their XP, levels, currencies, top items and rare drops.
- **Options:** the panel, and putting it back in its first spot.

## Time away

Offline progress is not counted in the per-hour rates. When the game catches up on time you were away, including after loading the game, what changed shows on its own as **Last time away**.

## Panel

A small panel with the skills you are training (XP per hour, time to the next level) and GP per hour. It starts bottom-left, beside the skill list. Drag it anywhere and it stays there. "–" folds it to one line. Click it to open the Logbook.

A session starts when you load a character and ends when you close the game or press **Start a new session**.
